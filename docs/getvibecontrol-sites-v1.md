## SECTION 4 — How it actually works

### Three steps. No code, no templates, no designer queue.

**1. Tell your AI worker what you need.**
A landing page for a campaign. A signup form for a workshop. A member portal for your course. A storefront for a digital product. Describe it in plain English. The way you’d describe it to a freelancer — except shorter, because the AI doesn’t need a project brief, it needs a goal.

**2. It builds the surface and shows you.**
Not a wireframe. Not a mockup. The actual working surface — copy, layout, design, forms wired up, integrations connected to the rest of your business. Built around the context your AI workforce already has about your business (because your Roleforge Blueprint defined that context once, and every worker reads from it).

**3. You review, refine, and ship.**
Like or don’t like the headline? Say so. The page updates. Want a different layout? Ask. Want to A/B test two versions? It sets that up too. When you’re ready, you ship. The page is yours, on your domain, hosted on VibeControl infrastructure.

What you don’t do: pick a template, choose fonts, hire a designer, write a brief, file a ticket, wait for revisions, fight with breakpoints, hire someone to fight with breakpoints for you.

## SECTION 6 — Common questions about Sites specifically

### A short FAQ — the rest is on the main pricing and FAQ pages.

**Is this a website builder?**
Not in the way you mean. A website builder is a tool you use. VibeControl Sites is something your AI workforce uses. You don’t learn the tool — your AI worker already knows it. You describe what you want; the worker builds it.

**Where do the sites actually live?**
On VibeControl’s infrastructure, served from `sites.vibecontrol.app` or your own custom domain (your call). Fast, secure, no servers for you to manage.

**Can I bring my own domain?**
Yes. Point your domain at VibeControl and your sites serve from there. SSL handled automatically.

**Can I edit the HTML / CSS directly?**
You can, if you want to — but most owners don’t. Editing by conversation is faster than editing by code for the kind of changes most businesses actually make.

**What about SEO?**
Standard SEO fundamentals — clean HTML, semantic structure, fast load times, proper meta tags — are how the AI worker builds by default. For more advanced SEO work, your AI worker handles the changes you describe.

**Is this just for landing pages, or full websites?**
Both. Landing pages, marketing sites, member portals, course platforms, simple storefronts, lead forms, event pages. If it’s a customer-facing surface that lives on the web, your AI workforce can build it.

**What if my business needs something genuinely custom?**
The Virtual CTO offering (through Build3r) handles custom implementations for businesses that need them. Most businesses don’t. For the small percentage that do, that path exists.

# Page-level notes for the developer/designer

**Page structure:**

- Inherits the same nav and footer as the getvibecontrol.com homepage
- Same primary CTA button design and text — never varied
- Sticky CTA bar appears at top after the user scrolls past the hero (same behavior as homepage)
- Showroom examples in Section 3 should be the visual heart of the page — generous space, clean annotations, real screenshots only
- Page should be noticeably shorter than the homepage (~7 sections vs ~12 on the homepage)

**Visual direction:**

- Same design system as getvibecontrol.com homepage and roleforgeai.com (consistent brand)
- Section 3 showroom is the most visually rich part of the page — treat it like an editorial feature, not a feature list
- All screenshots are real product output. Do NOT use AI-generated mockups or stylized illustrations of websites. This is non-negotiable for the same reasons articulated in the meta-ad-creative-concepts doc — AI-generated UI screams “AI-generated” and destroys the credibility this page is trying to build.
- Annotations on screenshots should be human-handwritten in tone, not corporate marketing copy

**What’s deliberately on this page:**

- Real screenshots of real surfaces from real customers
- Single CTA repeated throughout, funneling to Roleforge
- Capability-specific FAQ (the broad platform FAQ stays on the homepage)
- One paragraph (Section 5) connecting Sites back to the broader Business OS thesis
- siteai.chat → /sites redirect is the unmentioned-but-implicit reason this page exists

**What’s deliberately NOT on this page:**

- The phrase "SiteAI" anywhere (retired brand)
- Standalone pricing for Sites (it's not sold standalone; pricing is on the homepage)
- A signup or trial form (everyone funnels through Roleforge)
- Feature comparison with Webflow / Framer / Wix / Squarespace (we don't compete in their category)
- Technical detail about how the AI generates the code (the buyer doesn't care; this is a buyer-facing page)
- A separate waitlist (one funnel)
- Mention of other capabilities by name beyond Section 5's brief reference
- A "live demo" or "try it now" button (the demo is the Roleforge experience)

**SEO basics:**

- Meta title: "AI builds your website — VibeControl Sites"
- Meta description: "Your AI workforce builds and runs your customer-facing surfaces. Landing pages, member portals, forms, storefronts. Part of VibeControl. Start with a $47 Blueprint."
- Schema markup: SoftwareApplication (parent: VibeControl)
- Page should load in <2s on mobile (lazy-load showroom screenshots below the fold)
- Canonical URL: `https://getvibecontrol.com/sites`
- Redirects from siteai.chat: 301 to `https://getvibecontrol.com/sites`

**A/B test candidates (post-launch):**

- Hero headline: declarative ("Your website... built by your AI workforce") vs. provocative ("Stop briefing designers. Start describing outcomes.")
- Section 5 length: short Business OS connection vs. longer thesis explanation
- Showroom example order: agency first vs. course creator first (lean toward whichever archetype matches your highest-converting buyer once data exists)
- CTA destination: Roleforge homepage vs. a Sites-specific Roleforge entry point (only worth testing if Sites becomes a meaningful traffic source)

**Why this page is shorter than the homepage:**

- The homepage carries the full story; this page is one chapter, not the whole book
- A long capability page competes with the homepage for attention
- Visitors arriving here already have some context (they clicked into "Sites" from somewhere)
- Capability pages should each feel like a single, focused argument — not a parallel sales pitch
